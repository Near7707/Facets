# Facets

**Facets** is a SillyTavern extension that gives each character and persona a *wardrobe*: keep several pictures ("outfits") and change them per chat or for the whole character.

An outfit doesn't have to be clothes — a time of day, a place, a season, an injury, an age or an art style can each be one outfit.

## Features

- **Several outfits per character and persona.** Add many pictures at once; names come from the file names and you can fix them later.
- **Change in one tap.** Pick *This chat only* or *Whole character* once, then tap an outfit.
  - Every message avatar in the chat changes, including older messages.
  - *Whole character* applies to all of that character's chats, including new ones.
- **Default look** brings back the original avatar.
- **Crop first.** When you add pictures, each one opens in the crop screen (or tap *Skip* to use the automatic crop). Drag to move, and zoom *out* to fit the upper body (or more) into the small avatar. The frame follows SillyTavern's avatar shape: square for Circle/Square/Rounded, tall (2:3) for Rectangle. The chat shows the cropped picture; SillyTavern's zoomed avatar shows the full picture.
- **Matching sets.** Pair a character outfit with a persona outfit; changing one changes the other.
- **Drawers.** Group outfits into drawers such as "Casual" or "Formal" (one outfit can be in several). Drawer tabs filter the wardrobe; outfits added while a drawer tab is open go into that drawer.
- **From now on.** With *This chat only* you can turn on *From now on*: older messages keep the older outfit and only messages after the change show the new one (by each message's send time; the chat file is not touched). *History* lists the changes in this chat and lets you delete a wrong one. A branched chat starts with the outfits and history of the chat it came from.
- **Change words (automatic).** Give an outfit words like "suit, tie". When an AI reply uses one, the look changes from that reply on (older messages keep their outfit). Words on a drawer pick a random outfit inside it (nothing changes if one from that drawer is already worn). English matches whole words ("suit" does not match "suitcase"); Korean matches inside words, so particles are fine. A word right next to "take off", "without", "벗", "대신" and the like is ignored (the list can be edited in ⚙). A short notice appears; tap it to undo. A swipe or regenerate checks the new reply again.
- **Random.** 🎲 picks a random outfit from the open drawer tab, from now on in this chat. *Random each reply* does it for every new AI reply in this chat (a swipe picks again, so the history does not pile up).
- Automatic changes change the picture only (the AI is not told) and, by default, do not change the matching-set partner (a switch in ⚙ turns that on). Each chat keeps the last 200 changes per character/persona.
- **In the chat.** In the wardrobe, open *✨ Decorate the chat* (it opens the Photo Booth) and turn on *🧍 Current outfit picture* to place the picture of the current outfit over the chat (character and persona; it follows outfit changes). Drag it anywhere below the top bar — on a PC also into the empty space beside the chat. The input bar always stays on top, so a picture moved down looks like it stands behind it. A transparent-background full-body picture works best; a *Circle* border (round cut) suits pictures with a background. Pinch it (PC: mouse wheel) to resize, or tap it and drag the small handle on its corner. Turn it freely: twist with two fingers, Shift + mouse wheel on a PC, or tap it and drag the round handle above it; 0°, 90° and 180° snap a little. The tap bar is small: size, opacity, then 90° turn buttons, 🔒 *Lock*, *Tuck aside* and *Remove*. A locked picture lets touches through to the chat; a tucked-aside one leaves a small tab at the edge. **📌 Pinned pictures:** in *Decorate the chat*, pin any outfit pictures (up to 6) into the current chat. They show only in that chat and don't change when you change outfits; each has its own place, size and turn. Remove one and pin the same outfit again in that chat, and it comes back as it was. *Save this layout* keeps the pinned pictures (and the avatar decos) of a chat by name, to load into another chat of the same character. Phones and PCs keep their own place, size and turn. Off for every character until you turn it on; ⚙ can turn all of them off and hide them while the phone keyboard is open.
- **Photo Booth.** A cute, kitschy room for decorating, opened with the *Wardrobe | Photo Booth* switch at the top (or *✨ Decorate the chat*):
  - *Decorate the chat*: everything about the pictures in the chat (current outfit picture, pinned pictures, layouts).
  - *Picture border*: each picture gets its own border — 9 patterns (plain, dots, check, stripes, dashes, lace, glitter, rainbow, hologram), 7 colours or your own, 3 thicknesses, and 5 shapes (square, circle, heart, star, or following the picture's outline). With a circle, heart or star, drag the picture in the preview to choose which part shows, and zoom with the slider, mouse wheel or two fingers (*Face area* jumps to the top of a tall picture). Add little sparkles that can twinkle (they stop when your device is set to reduce motion).
  - *Avatar deco*: decorations for the chat's message avatars — polaroid, lace, ribbon, heart, sparkle, rainbow, candy, stars, hologram. Per chat, the character and you apart. Branch chats start with the same decos. Only a decoration is drawn around SillyTavern's avatar; the avatar itself is not changed. (A theme that decorates the box around the avatar may overlap with it.)
  - *Stickers*: in *Decorate the chat* → *Stickers*, tap a sticker to stick it in the middle of the chat (up to 12 per chat, apart from the 6 outfit pictures). 11 basic stickers in three looks — *Jelly*, *Die-cut* and *Gloss* — with optional twinkling *Sparkle*; the look can be changed for each sticker later in its border screen. Upload your own pictures as *My stickers* (a transparent-background PNG works best); they are shared by every chat. Stickers move, resize, turn, lock and take borders just like outfit pictures, and are saved with layouts and backups.
  - *Photocard*: in the Photo Booth → *Photocard*, make a 55:85 photocard. Pick a background (colour, pattern, your own picture or clear), put on outfit pictures of the character or you, or your own PNGs (up to 8), and stickers (up to 12). Drag to move, two fingers or the mouse wheel to resize and turn (Shift + wheel turns), with undo and redo. Saved photocards stay in *My photocards* to edit again; download one as a PNG, or stick a copy in the chat as one of *My stickers*. An unsaved photocard is kept when you close the window.
  - Photocards, four-cut strips and a collect book are coming later.
- **Themes.** Pick a look in ⚙ or in the extension settings: *Follow SillyTavern* (default, your theme colours), *Holo*, *Clean* or *Diary*. The wardrobe, the Photo Booth and the tool bar over the chat all use it; light or dark follows your SillyTavern theme. On a phone the window fills the screen.
